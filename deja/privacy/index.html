Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · Updated October 24, 2026

Deja is built to keep what it sees on your iPhone. This policy explains what Deja handles, where it stays and the choices you have.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your account. Your name, email and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself) are stored in your iPhone's Keychain. If you use Sign in with Apple, Deja stores the identifier Apple provides and the name and email you choose to share.
  • Your memories. Camera frames from your glasses or iPhone are processed on your iPhone to recognize the items and places you teach Deja, including while Deja runs in the background. Deja keeps the resulting sightings, small face-blurred snapshots and a few seconds of silent, face-blurred video around the moments things are put down or seen (you can turn video off). Video recorded in the background is held as face-blurred stills until you next open Deja, then turned into a clip. None of this leaves your iPhone.
  • Your voice questions. Questions you ask by voice are turned into text on your device. Audio is never recorded or stored.

Deja has no servers of its own. We don't receive, sell or share your account, your memories or your questions, and Deja contains no advertising or tracking.

Meta glasses

Deja connects to your glasses through Meta's Wearables Device Access Toolkit and the Meta AI app. Deja turns off the toolkit's analytics and crash reporting. Approving and connecting your glasses happens through the Meta AI app, under the Meta Privacy Policy. Your glasses' capture light is always on while the camera is in use.

Apple

If you use Sign in with Apple or Face ID, Apple handles that authentication under the Apple Privacy Policy. Deja never sees your Face ID data.

Permissions

Deja asks for Bluetooth and local network access to reach your glasses, the camera to learn items with your iPhone, the microphone and speech recognition for voice questions, and Face ID to confirm password resets. You can change these at any time in iOS Settings.

Your choices

  • Pause memory at any time, or turn off evidence video in Privacy.
  • Delete your history, everything Deja remembers, or your account in Privacy.
  • Deleting Deja removes all of its data from your iPhone.
  • To stop using Sign in with Apple, go to iOS Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple.

Children

Deja is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. See also our Terms of Use.
