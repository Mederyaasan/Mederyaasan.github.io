Deja

Never lose your things again.

Deja remembers where you put your keys, wallet and everything else, using your Meta glasses. Just ask “Where are my keys?”

Private by design. Everything Deja sees is processed on your iPhone. No images go to the cloud, faces are blurred, and audio is never recorded.

Works with Ray-Ban Meta, Oakley Meta and Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses, through the Meta AI app.
