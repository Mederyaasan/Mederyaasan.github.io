Connecting your glasses

  1. Install the Meta AI app and pair your glasses in it.
  2. In Deja, tap Connect Meta glasses, then Continue in Meta AI.
  3. Allow Deja in Meta AI. You'll return to Deja automatically.

If Meta AI shows an error, update Meta AI and your glasses' software, keep the glasses on and close to your iPhone with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on, then try again.

Forgot your password

On the log-in screen, tap Forgot password? and confirm it's you with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Your account lives on your iPhone, so there's no email to wait for.

Deleting your data

In Deja, open Privacy. You can delete your sighting history, everything Deja remembers, or your account. Deleting the app removes all of it from your iPhone.
